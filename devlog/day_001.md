## What is ray tracing?

Ray tracing is basically following a ray of light as it bounces and interacts
with objects.

Some key components: 

- Cameras, where the scene is being viewed from.
- Ray-object intersections, basically linear algebra.
- Light sources, there are many types of lights and sources!
- Visibility, is an object visible at all from the camera?
- Light scattering at surfaces. That is, materials encode some properties of the objects in the scene.
- Indirect light transport, allowing light rays to bounce more than once.
- Ray propagation, what are the properties of the ambient space we're
scattering light through? Is it a foggy room? Or is it a black hole.
