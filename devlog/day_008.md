# Day 8! Implementing shapes

Some of the examples we are rendering now are hand rolling the ray-circle
intersection. This time we'll get round to doing this properly with generic
Shape-Ray intersections. This will pave the way for other geometric objects.

A shape carries a bounding box to allow for quickly ruling out ray intersections.
Intersecting a ray with a shape should produce the following:

- An intersection point $p$,
- A surface normal: $N$ at point $p$,
- A reflected ray: $r$.
